Two Options for IMG

Following the meeting with the Chairman of IMG, Purple Sand proposes two clear pathways for the project. Both options retain the existing project as the starting point, and both include Purple Sand arranging the financing and managing the relationship with financiers throughout the build to completion. Purple Sand would not act as construction Project Manager under either option.

1. Option 1 — Existing Permit: Finance and Build

Under Option 1, IMG proceeds with the existing permit, existing NSA and existing GBA substantially unchanged. Purple Sand's role would be specifically focused on project financing and financier relationship management, including:

  1. Review and finalise the funding feasibility.
  2. Establish the capital structure, including senior construction debt and required equity.
  3. Approach banks, non-bank lenders (preferring a tier 1 bank) and appropriate capital providers.
  4. Negotiate interest rates, fees, LVR/LTC, presales, covenants, guarantees and funding conditions.
  5. Coordinate financier due diligence and financial close.
  6. Coordinate the financial interface between IMG, its QS, valuer, lawyers and financiers.
  7. Manage IMG's relationship with the financiers throughout construction.
  8. Assist with financier reporting and construction drawdowns.
  9. Monitor funding covenants, debt requirements, equity requirements and facility headroom.
  10. Manage the financier relationship through completion, apartment settlements and repayment of project debt.

Purple Sand would not manage the construction project. IMG and its appointed project manager, builder, architect, QS and consultants would remain responsible for construction and physical project delivery.

The project can be taken directly to the financing stage, but on the current preliminary assumptions the existing scheme is effectively at break-even. This provides very limited protection against construction escalation, delays, variations, interest-rate changes or sales risk — though with a high equity injection and a debt-free title, it remains a viable path.

2. Option 2 — Three-Month Optimisation, Then Finance and Build

Option 2 includes everything in Option 1, but before finalising construction finance, Purple Sand undertakes a focused three-month commercial review aimed at materially improving the project feasibility. The existing permit, 17,900 m² NSA and 26,901 m² GBA remain the starting position. The three-month exercise focuses initially on three major opportunities.

1. GST Review

Engage specialist GST advisers to review the existing project structure, acquisition history, margin scheme, input tax credits, construction GST and settlement treatment.

Current simplified GST provision≈ $27.0M
Potential savingTo be established by specialist advice
Estimated review cost≈ $30,000 (subject to quotations)
No GST saving would be included in the feasibility until independently verified.

2. Basement Redesign

Work with the existing architect and appropriate engineers/QS to investigate eliminating or substantially reducing the basement and transferring car parking into the podium, potentially replacing some office/commercial space. The review would compare the value of any commercial area lost against savings in excavation, retention, waterproofing, structure, services, construction time and financing.

Potential gross saving to investigate≈ $30M
This amount must be independently verified before being incorporated into the final feasibility.

3. Sales and Marketing

Replace or restructure the conventional ≈7% sales and marketing model using the sales and digital marketing system successfully utilised by Purple Sand, subject to demonstrating appropriate sales velocity, pricing and financier acceptance. Proven pre-sales greatly reduce risk for both bank and developer.

Current 7% cost≈ $20.80M
Target 3.5% cost≈ $10.40M
Potential saving≈ $10.40M

5. Purple Sand Recommendation

Option 1 provides IMG with the fastest pathway to financing and construction but, on the present preliminary numbers, starts at approximately break-even. Option 2 retains the existing permit as the project's base position but uses three months to investigate approximately $40.4M of identifiable potential savings before any GST benefit is included.

The commercial proposition is therefore straightforward:

OPTION 1
  • Finance the existing project and proceed.
OPTION 2
  • Spend three months determining whether the existing project can be materially improved, then finance and proceed.

Purple Sand's role under either option remains clearly defined: arrange and structure the financing, negotiate with the financiers, and manage IMG's financier relationships throughout the build through to completion and repayment. Construction project management remains with IMG and its appointed project team.
